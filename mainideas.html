2. Finding Main Ideas & Supporting Details

Definition: Identifying the central thesis statement and the evidence, arguments, or small pieces of information backing it up under CEFR methodology.

Core Methods & Overview:

  • Finding Main Ideas: Locating topic sentences in opening paragraphs, differentiating core arguments from trivial details, and spotting supporting examples.
  • Finding Details: Focusing on important information, answering who, what, when, where, why, and how, and using keywords from questions.
  • CEFR Focus: Aligns with A2 and B1 competencies to help students find main ideas in short texts and identify key points.

Learning Outcome: Student effectively separates core themes and major arguments from trivia while extracting specific supportive details accurately.
